# 2.1.1 - three-binner `--preset slow`

`--preset slow` now runs three binners instead of one. The comparison that had reduced it to
COMEBin alone used an incorrect number.

## `--preset slow` = metaBAT2 + SemiBin2 + COMEBin

On the gold assembly the trio and COMEBin alone are tied, at 177 and 176 clean high-quality MAGs.
End to end on per-sample assemblies the trio recovers more:

| arm | high-quality | clean | CPU-hours | GPU-hours |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| COMEBin alone | 61 | 57 | 80.9 | 1.7 |
| **metaBAT2 + SemiBin2 + COMEBin** | **64** | **60** | 120.7 | 2.4 |

Gold-assembly contigs are long and have a clean composition signal, so a second binner adds less
than it does on a fragmented real assembly. Binner comparisons on gold assemblies alone therefore
understate ensembles.

**metaBAT2 as the third binner.** The best trio by clean-MAG union is COMEBin + SemiBin2 + MaxBin2,
at 182 against 181 for this trio. MaxBin2 costs 24.5 CPU-hours against about 1.5 for metaBAT2, and
only 37.7% of its bins pass GUNC; metaBAT2's bins are much cleaner.

Clean high-quality MAGs per binner on the gold assembly, and how many no other binner finds:

| binner | clean HQ MAGs | unique to it |
|---|---|---|
| COMEBin | 176 | 39 |
| SemiBin2 | 116 | 4 |
| VAMB | 80 | 0 |
| metaBAT2 | 35 | 0 |
| MaxBin2 | 33 | 1 |
| CONCOCT | 28 | 0 |

## Also in this release

Tier cost is read from the run's logs. GPU-hours are reported separately from CPU-hours.

## Changed defaults

`--preset slow` only. `--preset default` (metaBAT2 + SemiBin2) is unchanged.
